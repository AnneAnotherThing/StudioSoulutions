You asked, so I built it

The September round, all of it live

Every note you gave me, and exactly what happens now.

  1. “Can studios manage their own listings?”
    They can. The studio portal is live: a studio signs in with their suite number and their code, sees their own card, and edits it directly. Hours, photos, phone, booking link, socials, bio, and their offer, all on two tabs, My listing and My offer. Changes go live immediately, and you get an email showing the old value next to the new one, so any bad edit can be put straight back.

    There are three levels now: On the map, Listed, and Featured. The page only shows a studio the fields their level includes, and tells them plainly what moving up would add. Who sits at which level is your call, in the admin panel, and at Salon Plus everyone gets the everything tier.
  2. “How do they get their code without me reading it over the phone?”
    The moment you set a studio live, they get a welcome email: you're on the map, here's your card, and here's your suite code for the portal. It fires exactly once, never again on a later edit.
  3. “Can they ask a question or report a problem?”
    Bottom of the portal: a Send a question and a Report a problem button. Both land in the admin panel in their own lane, with Reply and Mark done, and you get the email with a [QUESTION] or [BUG] subject so it sorts.
  4. “The hours people type are all over the place.”
    The signup form asks properly now: tap-able day chips and two time pickers, with a little preview that builds the line as they tap. A “my days vary” option stays for the genuinely odd schedules. What lands with us is always clean, which is also what makes the “Open now” badge work.
  5. “Ask for facts, and offer to write the bio for them.”
    The old “Anything else?” box is now “The facts for your card”, and right under it they choose: Write it for me, we turn their facts into a short polished bio, or Use my words, their facts go on the card untouched. Their choice arrives with the lead, and the write-it button in the admin panel does the polishing from what they gave us.
  6. “The change form shouldn't demand their business and contact name.”
    It doesn't anymore. A change request needs their suite and what to change, nothing else. New sign-ups still ask for the names, because there we genuinely need them.
  7. “Put the person's name on the cards, and 301 and 302 are swapped on the map.”
    Every card in the app now shows who is behind the chair next to what they do, and searching a person's name finds their studio. And suites 301 and 302 are finally in their right places on the map, everywhere the map appears.
And your sign-up sheet is ready. A printable one-pager with a QR code straight to the form and paper lines for anyone who'd rather write. It's in the link library below, print as many as you like.

The questions that come up in the hallway

Short answers you can give on the spot, with where to go when the answer needs a screen.

What does each level actually include?

On the map is a pin and a name, nothing else. Listed adds a photo, their phone, and hours. Featured is everything: four photos, booking link, socials, bio, and the ability to post offers. What each level includes is a set of switches in the admin panel's Settings tab, so we can tune the ladder without touching code. At Salon Plus, everyone gets Featured.

A studio lost their code

It's in the admin panel on their listing, next to the portal note. Text it to them; it only unlocks their own card, so it's safe to send.

Portal or change form, which do I point them at?

If they have their code: the portal, they do it themselves and it's live instantly. If they don't, or they're not listed yet: the join form. The “Update my listing” side now only asks for their suite and what to change.

They edited something and now it looks wrong

Every portal edit sends us an email with the old value next to the new one, so nothing is ever lost. Tell me what to put back, or open their listing in the admin panel and fix it there.

How does a brand-new building get started?

I add the building in the admin panel, it gets its own app link like the demo's, their tenants sign up through the generic join form with the building's name on it, and their new studios start on whatever level we set as that building's default. The demo below is exactly this, already done once.

Something's broken

The Report a problem button in the portal is the fastest lane, it arrives flagged and lands in its own list in the admin panel. Or just text me. Either way it gets fixed, usually same day.
