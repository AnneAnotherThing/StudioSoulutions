Studio Soulutions — Pricing v1

First stab · 2026-05-30 · Software-only (QR/PWA + optional kiosk) · No hardware sold

Pricing strategy in one paragraph: charge the building owner when possible — it's a smaller sales cycle and a higher LTV. When the owner doesn't buy in, let tenants self-serve, with a free tier so the directory still gets populated (the product fails without listings). Kiosk software is the add-on, not the core — it makes the lobby presence real but the QR/PWA does the actual work.

Why these numbers (the gut check)

  • $149 lands in "yes, I can expense that" territory for a salon-suite operator running 25–35 suites. Per-suite, it's $4–6/month — invisible against the $400–700/mo each suite pays in rent.
  • $14 for tenant Promote sits below the price of a single client no-show — easy mental math for a stylist. Lower than $20 (the next psychological barrier).
  • Free "Listed" tier is non-negotiable. The directory has no value empty. Free tenants are the inventory that lets you sell to owners later.
  • $49 kiosk add-on is intentionally cheap as an upsell — its job is to make Studio Soulutions visible to walk-in clients, which sells the next building.
  • Tier 3 has "unlimited" not "+$X/suite" — usage-based pricing scares property managers; flat fees close deals.

Open questions before this goes public

  • Should the kiosk add-on be free on Connect Pro (Tier 3) to make the top tier feel premium?
  • Is there appetite for a one-time "white-glove onboarding" SKU (e.g., $499) for owners who want Anne to set up the building? Could be a meaningful revenue line early.
  • Do we cap tenant Featured slots per building (e.g., max 5 featured at any time) to keep the slot valuable?
  • Cross-building visibility on tenant Featured — premium feature or a way to push owners to subscribe (since their building gets surfaced elsewhere only if they're on a paid tier)?
  • Founding-member pricing for the first 10 paying buildings — lock in 50% off for life? Strong outbound hook.
