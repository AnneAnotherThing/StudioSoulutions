The product, on one page

Every part, every feature, one page.

The whole platform as a checklist: what each piece does and where it lives. Every building gets its own copy of each piece; the addresses below show Salon Plus Studios, the first live building, as the working example. Deep how-tos live in the building's rollout; this is the sheet to keep by the desk. Green-edged boxes are owner tools.

The app · for customers

studiosoulutions.com/salonplus/app
  • Search finds people: studio, person behind the chair, service, or suite number
  • Category chips plus Open now and Available now filters
  • Open now runs on real hours, Phoenix clock
  • Cards: photos and gallery, bio, Call, Text, Book, website, socials
  • Live specials on the card: an offer tag on the row, a Right now banner on the profile
  • Suite map with "Show me on the map" and the entrance-to-door glow
  • Claim button on unclaimed cards recruits tenants by itself
  • Installs like an app: Add to Home Screen, its own icon

The directory page · for the lobby

studiosoulutions.com/salonplus
  • Map plus full roster on one page, made for QR signs
  • Join section at #join for new studios
  • Already listed? pointer sends existing studios to their portal

The kiosk · for the lobby screen

studiosoulutions.com/kiosk
  • Attract loop when idle, a tap wakes it
  • Search, open-now pills, suite map, and a QR handoff to the phone

The join form · new studios only

studiosoulutions.com/salonplus/#join
  • Two minutes on a phone: identity, suite, phone or email
  • Hours done properly: day chips, time pickers, live preview line
  • Bio choice: Write it for me, or Use my words
  • Up to four photos straight from the camera roll
  • Instant receipt email that names what happens next
  • Old change links now land in the portal, nothing breaks

The studio portal · their own door

studiosoulutions.com/salonplus/offer
  • Sign in: suite + code, or the one-tap Edit your card link in the welcome email
  • Every studio has a code, whatever its level; the level only decides fields
  • Edit in place: hours, photos, contact, booking, bio, live on save
  • Offers: composed from safe pieces, blanket offers with no services, one live per studio, posting replaces, Take it down ends it early
  • Send a question / Report a problem buttons reach the inbox flagged
  • Safety net: every edit sends a before-and-after email
