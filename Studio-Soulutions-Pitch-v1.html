The whole experience

A lobby kiosk + a mobile companion, talking to the same building.

Two surfaces, one tenant database, zero duplicate work. The owner updates a suite once; both screens update everywhere.

Lobby kiosk

Touchscreen welcome wall. Visitors arrive, see who's inside, who's open right now, and where to go.

Mobile companion

Scan the QR. The lobby experience continues on their phone — book, save, and find them later.

AI-written bios

Tenants speak a sentence, drop a link, or paste rough notes. Studio Soulutions writes the polished version.

● Attract loop when idle
02 · Mobile

Take the lobby with you.

One scan and the building is on their phone — every suite, every artist, who's open, how to book. They walk in for nails and leave knowing about the lash room and the wellness suite they didn't see.
