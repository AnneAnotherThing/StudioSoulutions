03 · Yours, not ours

Fully customizable.
Branded to your building.

Studio Soulutions is the platform — your building is the brand. Drop in your logo, your palette, your photography, your voice. The kiosk in your lobby and the app on your visitors' phones look like they were built for you, because they were.

Same engine underneath. Different face on top of it. Every building keeps its own identity.

Your name, your mark

Your building's logo, wordmark, and favicon — across the kiosk, the app, and every guest's bookmark bar.

Your palette

Swap in your colors — every accent, button, header, and chip updates everywhere. No theme files to edit by hand.

Your photography

Your corridor on the hero. Your lobby in the attract loop. Your suites on the directory. We use your shots, not stock.

Your voice

From the welcome line to the "from the owner" note. Edit every word in the admin — no developer needed.

Your domain

Point your own URL at the app. Guests see app.yourbuilding.com, not a generic SaaS subdomain.

Your features

Turn modules on or off — community board, available suites, suite map, "Did you know?" rotator. Show only what fits your building.

The Studio Soulutions name never has to appear publicly. You can run it fully white-labeled — your brand front to back, your guests never see ours.
