The problem

A building full of artists. A printed directory taped to the wall.

Salon-suite buildings have a discovery problem. A guest walks in for a haircut and never finds out about the lash room, the wellness suite, the nail studio two doors down. Each tenant runs their own business — which is the whole point — but the building, as a whole, never gets to introduce itself.

Studio Soulutions changes that without changing how anyone runs their suite.

How it works

An app in their pocket. Optionally, a kiosk in the lobby.

The mobile companion ships on every install. The lobby kiosk is a premium add-on for buildings that want the front door itself to feel like the brand.

Questions

The ones we usually get.

Do my tenants have to do anything?

No. The owner runs the directory. Tenants can submit their own photos, bio, and hours through a self-serve link, but they don't have to. You can populate everything yourself if that's how your building works.

What hardware do I need for the kiosk?

Any modern touchscreen with a browser works — a wall-mounted iPad, a 32" portrait display with a mini PC, a Surface Hub. The kiosk is a premium add-on; we can help you pick something that fits your lobby and looks the part.

Is the kiosk required?

No. The mobile companion ships on every install — that's the standard offering. The lobby kiosk is a premium add-on for buildings that want the front door itself to feel like the brand. You can start with the app and add the kiosk later.

How customizable is the look?

Fully. Logo, wordmark, color palette, photography, copy, even the welcome message from the owner — all yours. The Studio Soulutions name never has to appear publicly. You can run it white-labeled at your own domain.

Does it replace my tenants' booking systems?

No. Each tenant keeps using whatever they already use — Vagaro, Square, GlossGenius, a paper book. The "Book" button on their card links straight to their existing system. We're the discovery layer, not the booking layer.

How long does setup take?

Once we have your logo, palette, and tenant list, the app goes live within a week. The kiosk add-on takes a little longer — hardware sourcing and install — but we handle all of it. You don't chase anything.

What does it cost?

Pricing depends on building size and feature set. We're working with first buildings right now to get the model right — reach out and let's talk about what fits.
