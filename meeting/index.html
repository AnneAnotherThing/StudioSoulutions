The meeting walkthrough

Demo the demo, then hand over the keys.

The whole walk happens in The Beauty Collective, the demo building, so nothing touches Salon Plus until the reveal at the end. Tick as you go; every stop has its link. Phone for the first half, laptop for the admin half, paper to finish.

Part one · the app, in the demo building

Open the demo app on your phone. One building, one screen. Search a studio by name, then by a service like nails.
/a/demo
Open Bloom's card. Photos and gallery, bio, hours, and the buttons: Call rings them, Book opens their own booking system. Nothing replaces how a studio already works.
Show me on the map. From the card, the suite glows on the floor plan, entrance to door. Every building gets its own traced map.
Open now, for real. The badge runs on actual hours, live clock. Which is why the hours question matters on every form.
Specials, everywhere they sell. The Specials tab, the offer tag riding a studio's row, the Right now banner on the profile.
The claim button. A bare pin carries “This is my studio”: the app recruits tenants by itself.

Part two · a studio's life, start to finish

The join form, on the phone. Day chips composing the hours line, the bio choice, photos from the camera roll. Two minutes, from their own suite.
/join/?building=The Beauty Collective
Sign into the portal as Bloom. Change the hours, save, refresh the app: live. Post an offer, then Take it down. Studios run their own cards; the tier only decides fields.
/portal/?b=demo
The safety net. Every portal edit emails a before-and-after, so nothing changes without the owner knowing, and any edit is a two-minute undo.
Already listed? The pointers on the directory and join form, and the old texted change links, all land a studio in its own portal.

Part three · the owner's side, on the laptop

The admin panel, filtered to the demo building. Passcode in person. The six sections, then the job that matters: publish the sign-up from part two and watch the welcome email go out with the code and the one-tap edit link.
/leads
The write-the-bio button. Publishing a write-up lead opens the studio's drawer; one tap, three bios in different tones.
Codes without fear. Every studio's portal code on its listing, copy buttons, New code if one leaks. Hide and Delete both exist; hide is the everyday one.

Part four · the reveal: Salon Plus already has all of it

Open the Salon Plus app. Everything from the demo is live for Salon Plus today: the real roster, the corrected map.
/salonplus/app
Install it together. Add to Home Screen: the building, its own icon, one tap from now on.
The owner hub, saved next to it. Every link, live counts, the copy-paste texts for photos, hours and socials, and the promise that every change writes itself up there.
/laura
Paper in hand. The rollout, eleven pages, the reference (page 9 is the first week; page 11 the cut-out QRs). Portal cards, one per studio with its code. The sign-up sheet for the front desk.
/salonplus/rollout · /leads/portal-cards · /signup-sheet.html
