In the lobby · 01

Lobby kioskLive

A touchscreen welcome wall that lives in the corridor. Visitors arrive, see who's inside, who's open, and where to go — instead of squinting at a printed directory. Boots into an attract loop with the corridor photo so the screen is never dark.

Highlights

  • Big "Salon Plus Studio Suites" welcome over the actual corridor photo.
  • Four glassy menu cards: Directory, Open right now, Suite map, From the owner.
  • QR card top-right so guests can take the experience with them.
  • Real-photo tenant tiles (Dueces is the only confirmed one; others are marked "Test").
  • Attract loop with photo crossfade and a 60-second idle-to-sleep timer.
  • Reads tenants from the admin so updates appear automatically.

Mobile companionLive

Behind the scenes · 03

Owner adminLive

Where you run the building. Log in, add a tenant with their photo and bio, mark them active or coming soon, and see them appear on the kiosk and the phone within seconds. Use the AI bio generator to draft three tone variants from a few rough notes.

Highlights

  • Demo mode — type anything as the email and password to walk in.
  • Tenant CRUD with photo upload, service tags, hours, status, contact info.
  • AI bio generator with Warm / Editorial / Friendly tones. Wired to call Claude through a Netlify Function once ANTHROPIC_API_KEY is set; falls back to a smart stub locally.
  • "Mark as test/concept" toggle so concept cards stay obviously labeled.
  • Available Suites tab — list vacant rooms with sqft, rent, and an available-from date. Shows up on the phone as its own splash card.
  • Highlights tab for welcomes and building news that appear on the kiosk.
  • Building tab for studio name, tagline, owner note, contact info.
  • Adds + edits sync to the kiosk and phone via shared local storage.
