Your building, in everyone's pocket.

Salon Plus Studios is full of independent artists, and until now the only way a visitor met them was to walk past their doors. Studio Soulutions changes that: one app that carries every studio in the building, findable, mappable, and bookable, without changing how anyone runs their suite. This guide is the whole system on paper: what it is, what it does for your studios and for you, and how the day-to-day works.

What it never touches

Each studio keeps its own booking system, its own prices, its own way of working. The app is the discovery layer, not the booking layer: the Book button on a card goes straight to whatever that studio already uses. Nobody has to change anything to be part of it.

What your studios get.

Free to them, which is worth saying out loud, because everyone assumes there's a catch. Here is what being on the app actually does for a studio.

A card that sells for them

Up to four photos, their hours, phone, booking link, Instagram and socials, and a short bio. The card is how a stranger in the lobby becomes a client. A studio with photos gets looked at; one without gets scrolled past.

Found by name, person, or service

Search works on the studio's name, the person behind the chair, and what they do. "Lash", "Christina", or "Deuces" all land in the right place. Their card also shows Open now when their real hours say so.

Walk-in traffic they never met

Every card cross-promotes the rest of the building: someone here for nails learns there's a barber, a day spa, a lash room two doors down. The building sells itself sideways, and every studio is part of that.

Specials, posted by them

From the portal, a studio composes an offer from safe building blocks: dollars off, percent off, or a free add-on with a service they actually offer. It's live in the app instantly, expires on its own, and one offer per studio keeps the feed tidy.

And they run it themselves

With their suite number and their code, a studio signs into the portal and manages their whole card: hours, photos, contact, socials, bio, offer. Saves go live immediately. No waiting on anyone, no phone tag. If they'd rather not, the paper way still works: tell the front desk what to change, and it gets done for them.

The pitch in one breath: "We're putting every studio in the building on one app and map, so customers can actually find you. It's free. Two minutes to get on it."

What you get.

The building finally gets to introduce itself. Every tenant discovered, not just the ones with a sign near the door, and a set of quiet levers that make the whole place easier to fill and run.

Happier tenants who stay

A studio that gets walk-ins from the app has one more reason to renew. The cross-promotion between studios is traffic none of them can buy alone; it only exists because the building acts as one.

A recruiting engine

Every unclaimed card carries "This is my studio, claim my free card." The join link and the printable sign-up sheet bring new studios in, and vacancies can be listed right in the app for prospective tenants to see.

Your brand, front to back

The app wears Salon Plus Studios: your name, your look, your building's real hallway photos. The platform name never has to appear to your customers.

Levels you control

Three levels decide what a card shows: On the map (a pin and a name), Listed (photo, phone, hours), and Featured (everything, plus offers). What each level includes is a set of switches in the admin panel, and today every Salon Plus studio gets Featured.

And almost none of it is your job

Anne runs the system: builds and polishes the cards, checks the suite against the map, publishes, fixes anything odd, and keeps your hub page current. Your part is the one thing nobody else can do: being the trusted face who hands a studio the link. A text from you gets a form filled the same day; a poster on a wall gets ignored.

Your one link to keep: the hub page has every link, every answer, and the exact words to text a studio, and it updates itself whenever anything changes. It's private to you: studiosoulutions.com/laura

How a studio gets on, start to finish.

Worth knowing cold, because it's the question you'll get in the hallway. The colored words say whose move it is: yours or ours.

You hand them the link

A text, usually. They fill it in because you asked, not because a poster told them to. This is the whole of your part.

They fill in the form, from their phone

Two minutes, standing in their own suite: name, suite, tap-able day and time pickers, services, up to four photos, and their choice on the bio: "write it for me" or "use my words." They also pick their portal code right there.

A receipt lands in their inbox, instantly

Automatic. "We got your details, someone's on it." No awkward silence where they wonder if it worked.

The request lands with us

In the admin Inbox and by email, labeled by kind: a new studio, a change to an existing listing, a question, or a problem report. Each looks different, so nothing gets buried.

We build the card and publish

Tidy the wording, crop the photos, check the suite against the map, publish. Nothing goes live in your building without this step. Usually same day.

Their welcome email arrives, code inside

The moment the card goes live, once and only once: "you're on the map, here's your card, and here's your portal sign-in." From then on they self-serve.

They keep their own card fresh

Portal edits go live instantly, and every edit sends us a before-and-after email, so a bad change can always be put straight back. Nothing is ever lost.

Already listed, no code handy? The change form needs only their suite and what to change: studiosoulutions.com/salonplus/?change · It reaches us flagged as a change, never muddled with new sign-ups.

The admin panel, in five minutes.

One page, five tabs, at studiosoulutions.com/leads. It asks for the passcode, which Anne gives you in person, never in print. You genuinely cannot break anything: every change is logged, and anything can be put back.

TabWhat it's for
StudiosEvery listing in the building. Open one to edit anything on its card, see its portal code, or change its status: draft, live, or hidden.
InboxEverything studios send in: new sign-ups to publish, change requests to apply, questions and problem reports to answer, each in its own lane.
OffersEvery live special. Nothing here needs approval; the one control is Hide, for the rare offer that shouldn't be up.
SettingsThe level switches: what On the map, Listed, and Featured each include. Change a switch, and every card obeys it on the next load.
ChangesThe ledger. Who changed what, and when, for every edit made anywhere in the system.

Job one: publish

Inbox, open the new sign-up, check it over, publish at a level. That's the moment their card goes live and their welcome email fires.

Job two: fix a card

Studios tab, open the listing, change the thing, save. Or just tell Anne; either way it's minutes, not days.

Job three: lost code

It's on their listing in the Studios tab. Text it over; it only opens their own card, so it's safe to send.

Put it on your phone.

The app installs to the home screen like any app: its own icon, full screen, one tap from then on. These steps work for you, your studios, and any customer who asks.

Android (Chrome)

  • Scan the code, or open the app link
  • Tap the ⋮ menu, top right
  • Tap Install app (or "Add to home screen")
  • Confirm. The Salon Plus icon appears

iPhone (Safari)

  • Scan the code, or open the app link
  • Tap the Share button, bottom center
  • Tap Add to Home Screen
  • Tap Add. The Salon Plus icon appears

Worth teaching your studios

A studio that installs the app sees the building the way their customers do, and the portal is one more tap from there. It also makes them your best advertisers: "here, scan this" travels fast when it's already on their own phone.
