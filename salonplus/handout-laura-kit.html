Quick pitch, three beats
  • 1. "You're already on the map", open the directory, show them their suite. People light up when they see their name.
  • 2. "The listing is free", the form takes two minutes, paper or phone, whichever they like.
  • 3. "If you want more", full card with photos, bio, and booking link. That's the pricing sheet. No pressure on day one; free yeses first.
