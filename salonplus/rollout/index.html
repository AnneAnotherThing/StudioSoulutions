The app: finding people.

Open studiosoulutions.com/salonplus/app on any phone. This is what a customer sees, and everything on this page is worth trying yourself before you show anyone.

The Discover screen

The front door. A welcome that counts the building's studios, a search bar, and big cards: Directory (everyone), Open right now (who's open this minute, and who can take you), and the Suite map. Below them: a featured studio that rotates daily, available suites when any are listed, and a note from the owner.

Search finds people, not just studios

The search bar matches the studio's name, the person behind the chair, the services, and the suite number. "Christina", "barber", "lash", or "103" all land in the right place. Every card also shows the person's name next to the studio's, because people come back to people.

The Directory, with filters

The full roster as a scrolling list. Chips across the top narrow it: All, Open now, Available now, and the service categories. Open now means the doors are open; Available now means open and not marked busy with a client.

"Open now" runs on real hours

A card only earns the live Open badge when we have the studio's actual opening and closing times, not just their days. That is why the hours question matters so much on the forms: no times, no badge, ever. The badge checks the clock in Phoenix time.

How to: answer "who does lashes here?"
  1. Open the app, type "lash" in the search bar
  2. Every matching studio appears with its suite number; tap one
  3. The card shows their photos, hours, and a Book or Call button
  4. Tap "Show me on the map" and the suite lights up on the floor plan
How to: see who could take a walk-in right now
  1. Discover screen, tap Open right now
  2. The Directory opens pre-filtered; the count line says how many are open and how many can take you now
  3. Switch the chip to Available now to hide anyone marked "with a client"

The app: cards, the map, specials.

Anatomy of a card

  • Hero photo plus a small gallery; tap a gallery shot to see it full screen
  • Name, person, suite, hours, and the live status pill
  • Bio: one to three warm sentences
  • Action buttons: Book (their own system), Call, Text, website, email, whichever they've listed
  • Social icons: Instagram, Facebook, TikTok
  • "While you're at...": other open studios, the cross-promotion moment

The claim button

A card nobody has claimed yet carries a dashed button: "This is my studio, claim my free card." It leads straight to the join form. The app recruits your tenants for you, right at the moment they're looking at their own half-empty card. Once claimed, the button disappears; customers never see it on a finished card.

The map, and "show me on the map"

The Map tab draws the real building: both wings, the breezeway, the entrance star. Suites with studios are filled; tap one to open its card. From any card, Show me on the map flips to the map with that suite glowing, so "where is she?" is answered by pointing at a lit room. Start at the entrance pin, walk to the glow.

The Specials tab

Every live offer in the building, newest first, each with its expiry date. Offers come from the studios themselves through their portal (page 5), are built from safe pre-set pieces, and take themselves down when they expire. Nothing here needs your approval.

How to: put the app on a phone like a real app
  1. Android (Chrome): open the app link, tap the ⋮ menu, tap Install app, confirm. The Salon Plus icon lands on the home screen
  2. iPhone (Safari): open the app link, tap Share, tap Add to Home Screen, tap Add
  3. Either way it opens full screen with its own icon from then on. Teach every studio this; it makes them your best advertisers
Also in the family: the directory page at studiosoulutions.com/salonplus is the map and full roster on one page, made to sit behind a QR code on lobby signs and flyers. It explains the building to someone who has never set foot in it, then hands them to the app.

Getting a studio on the app.

The join form is for new studios only: studiosoulutions.com/salonplus/#join. A studio that is already listed never fills a form again; they change their own card in the portal (page 5), live the moment they save. The old texted change link, studiosoulutions.com/salonplus/?change, now lands them on the portal sign-in, so every link you've ever sent still works.

The join form, field by field

  • Business and contact name: required for a new studio, that's how the card gets its identity
  • Suite, phone or email (one of the two, so we can reach them)
  • Hours, done properly: tap-able day chips and two time pickers, with a little preview that composes the line as they tap. "My days vary" exists for the odd schedules. Clean hours are what power the Open now badge
  • Services: tap to choose, these become their search tags and offer menu
  • Socials, booking link, website: whatever they have
  • The facts for your card, then their choice: Write it for me (we turn facts into a polished bio) or Use my words (their text, untouched)
  • Photos: up to four, straight from the camera roll

Already listed? The portal, not a form

Existing studios edit their own card directly: hours, photos, booking link, bio, all live on save, with the before-and-after email as the safety net. The join form and the directory both carry an "Already listed?" pointer that sends them there. Lost code? They can ask the developer: anne@hive-rise.com, and it's back in a minute.

What happens the moment they hit send

  1. They get an instant email receipt: "we got it, someone's on it"
  2. It lands in the admin Inbox and in the email, labeled by kind
  3. We build or fix the card and publish: nothing appears in your building without that step
  4. On first publish, their welcome email fires, once, with their card link, their portal code, and a one-tap Edit your card button
Your part is step zero: handing them the link. A text from you gets a form filled the same day. The exact words to send are on your hub, ready to copy.
How to: get a brand-new studio on, start to finish
  1. Text them: "We're putting every studio on one app and map, so customers can actually find you. It's free. Two minutes: studiosoulutions.com/salonplus/#join"
  2. They fill it in from their suite, photos and all
  3. You or Anne publish it from the admin Inbox (page 7), usually same day
  4. Their welcome email delivers their portal code; they're live on the map and running their own card

The studio portal.

Each studio's own door, at studiosoulutions.com/salonplus/offer. Every studio has one, whatever its level: the level decides which fields they can edit, never whether they can get in. They sign in with their suite number and their code, which arrived in their welcome email and is printed on the portal cards you hand out, or they tap the Edit your card button in that same email and they're signed straight in, no typing. A code opens only that studio's card, so it's safe to text to its owner.

The My listing tab

Their card, editable: hours, photos, phone and whether texting is okay, booking link, website, email, socials, bio. Saves go live in the app immediately. The page only shows each studio the fields their level includes, and tells anyone below Featured what moving up would add.

Nothing can be quietly broken

Every portal edit sends us an email showing the old value next to the new one. A bad edit is a two-minute restore, never a mystery. If a studio says "it looked better before," the before is sitting in the inbox.

The My offer tab

Specials are composed, not typed. They pick a shape: dollars off, percent off, or free add-on with one of their own services, then how long it runs (14 to 90 days, no forever option) and who it's for. A short fine-print line is allowed; links, phone numbers and shouting are stripped automatically. One live offer per studio; posting a new one replaces the old; five changes a day at most; and a Take it down button ends their own offer early, no waiting on anyone.

Send a question · Report a problem

Two buttons at the bottom of the portal. Whatever they write lands in the admin Inbox in its own lane and reaches the email flagged [QUESTION] or [BUG]. It's the fastest path from "something's off" to "fixed," and it saves your hallway from becoming the ticket system.

How to: walk a studio through their first special
  1. Portal, sign in, My offer tab
  2. Pick the shape: say, "$10 off" plus their "Gel manicure" service
  3. Pick 30 days and "New clients," add fine print if any, post
  4. Open the app's Specials tab together: it's already there, expiry shown. It takes itself down on day 30
Lost code? It's on their listing in the admin panel's Studios tab. Text it over, done. Leaked, or lost beyond finding? New code on that same listing mints a fresh one and the old one stops working the same second.

The sharp edges, and the fixes.

The handful of rules worth knowing cold, then the what-to-check list for the day something looks wrong. Almost nothing in this system is destructive; the few things with edges are all here.

Hide first; delete is permanent

Hide is the everyday choice: off the app, everything kept (photos, hours, code), and Restore brings it back as a draft for a once-over. Studios leave buildings and come back; hide assumes so. Delete studio, in the card's drawer, is for the truly gone: the card and its portal code are removed for good, the deletion is logged in Changes, and there is no undo.

Level changes bite immediately, but gently

Turn photos off for a level and those cards lose their photos on the next load, no deploy. Nothing is deleted: the photos sit on file and return the moment the switch flips back.

Photos have two rules

Photos sent with a change request are not applied automatically: someone puts them on the listing, so a blurry shot can never silently replace a good one. And in the studio editor, tap any photo to make it the profile; tick "show whole, uncropped" for business cards and logos that must not crop.

If a code leaks

Worst case is one studio's card edited or a fake offer posted, nothing wider. Hide any bad offer, open the studio in the panel, press New code: a fresh code is minted and the old one stops working the same second. Text them the new one and it's over.
