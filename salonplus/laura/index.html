New since you asked

Everything you asked for is live

Four questions, four answers. Nothing here needs anything from you — it's already running.

  1. “If a studio wants to add pictures, do we have an edit form?”
    There is now. The join form has two buttons at the top — I'm new here and Update my listing — so a studio that's already on the app can send changes without starting over. Photos went from 3 to 4, and the first one is their profile shot.
  2. “Can we add Instagram, Facebook and website buttons?”
    Yes. The form now asks for a website alongside Instagram, Facebook and TikTok, and the ones a studio gives us turn into round icon buttons on their card in the app.
  3. “Can they get a notification that their info is received?”
    They do, if they leave an email. A short receipt lands in their inbox the moment they submit — different wording for a new listing than for a change — so nobody is left wondering whether the form worked, and nobody texts you to ask.
  4. “A change request should reach you differently from a new studio.”
    It does, three ways. The email to Anne is subject-line tagged [CHANGE] so it sorts and searches, it arrives in a different colour with what they want changed at the very top, and in the admin panel change requests sit in their own section with a button that opens that studio's listing directly. Marked done when it's handled, not deleted.
The one link that does all of it: the “wants a change, or new photos” card below. It opens the form already switched to Update my listing, and if you pick a studio from the dropdown, it fills their suite in too.
