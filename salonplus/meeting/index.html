Walking Laura through it, in order.

Anne's meeting sheet. Tick as you go; every stop has its link. Phone in hand for the first half, her laptop for the admin half, paper at the end.

Part one · the app, on your phone

Open the app installed on your phone. Let it land: her building, one screen. Search “Christina”, a person, not a studio.
/salonplus/app
Open a full card. Photos, bio, hours, and the buttons: Call rings them, Book opens their own system. Nothing replaces how a studio works.
Show me on the map. Tap it from the card: the suite glows, entrance to door. Then tap 301 on the map, Deuces, second on the left, correctly.
Open now, for real. The badge runs on actual hours, Phoenix clock. This is why the hours ask matters.
Specials, everywhere they sell. The tab, the offer tag on a studio's row, and the Right now banner on their profile.
The claim button. Find an unclaimed card: the app recruits her tenants for her.
Install it on HER phone. Add to Home Screen together. Her building, her icon, one tap from now on.

Part two · a studio's life, in the demo building

The join form, on the phone. Day chips composing the hours line, the bio choice, photos from the camera roll. Two minutes, done from their suite.
/salonplus/#join
The studio portal, signed in as Bloom. Change the hours, save, refresh the app: live. Post an offer, then Take it down. Her studios run their own cards.
/portal/?b=demo
The safety net. Every portal edit emails a before-and-after. Nothing can change without her knowing, and any edit is a two-minute undo.
Already listed? The buttons on the directory and join form, and the old texted change links, all land studios in their portal now.

Part three · her side of the counter, on the laptop

The admin panel. Passcode in person. The six sections, then the one job that matters: publish a sign-up from the Inbox and watch the welcome email carry the code and the one-tap edit link.
/leads
The write-the-bio button. Publishing a write-up lead opens the studio's drawer; one tap, three bios in different tones.
Codes without fear. Every studio's portal code on its listing, copy buttons, and New code if one ever leaks. Hide and Delete both exist; hide is the everyday one.
Her hub, saved to her home screen. Every link, live counts, the copy-paste texts for photos, hours and socials, and the promise: every change writes itself up here.
/laura
