Coupon codes

What they are, when they matter, and when you can ignore them entirely.

What a code is
A short password for one studio, like DEUCES-4417. It lets that studio post its own coupon at studiosoulutions.com/salonplus/offer/ and nothing else. It cannot see other studios, edit their listing, or reach this panel.
Where it comes from
Made automatically the moment you publish a studio on a tier that allows coupons. You never write one by hand. It appears in a popup right after you hit Publish.
When you care
Only twice. Once, when you hand it to the studio so they can post offers. Again, if they lose it and ask.
When you can ignore it
Every other time. A studio that never posts a coupon simply never uses its code, and nothing breaks. Codes do not expire and there is nothing to renew.
If one leaks
The worst case is a fake offer on one studio's card. Hide it on the Offers tab, then move that studio to a tier without coupons and back again — that mints a fresh code and the old one stops working.
Dropping a tier
Move a studio to a tier that has coupons switched off and its code stops working straight away. Move it back and it works again.
What to send a studio:
"Here's your code for posting specials: YOUR-CODE. Go to studiosoulutions.com/salonplus/offer/, put in your suite number and that code, and you can post an offer in about a minute. It comes down on its own when it runs out."
