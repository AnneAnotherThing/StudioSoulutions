The manual

How all of this works

Every moving part of the Salon Plus system, in one place: what each piece is, who it's for, what every button in the admin panel does, and how to handle the jobs that come up week to week.

It's written to be read out of order. Skim the contents, jump to the bit you need, ignore the rest. Nothing here assumes you remember anything from last time.

1. The pieces, and who each one is for

Six things exist. Everything else is a detail of one of them.

For customers
The app

The mobile directory. Browse by name, service or floor map, then call, text or book. Shows an Open now badge when we hold a studio's actual opening and closing times, and shows any live offer on the studio's card.

studiosoulutions.com/salonplus/app/
For customers, and for signage
The directory page

The map plus the full roster on one page, with the join form at the bottom. This is the one to put behind a QR code on a lobby sign or a flyer.

studiosoulutions.com/salonplus/
For studios not yet listed
The join form

Name, suite, hours, services, socials, website, up to four photos. Free to the studio. Lands in the panel's Inbox as a new submission.

studiosoulutions.com/salonplus/#join
For studios already listed
The change form

The same form, opened already switched to Update my listing. Requires a suite number and a description of what to change. Lands in the Inbox as a change request, kept visually apart from new sign-ups.

studiosoulutions.com/salonplus/?change#join

Add a suite to pre-fill it for one studio: ?suite=4417&change#join

For studios on an offer tier
The offer form

Studios post their own specials using their suite number and their posting code. Built from dropdowns so nothing can go up looking wrong, and every offer expires on its own.

studiosoulutions.com/salonplus/offer/
For whoever runs the building
The admin panel

Where listings actually live. Studios, Inbox, Offers, Settings and a change log. Passcode protected, and every edit is recorded against whoever was signed in.

studiosoulutions.com/leads/
The short version of who does what: The building manager's whole job is getting the right link into a studio's hands. Everything after that happens in the panel, or happens by itself.

2. What happens when someone fills in a form

Same six steps every time, whether it's a new studio or a change.

1. They submit, from their phone

Photos are compressed in the browser before they're sent, so a bad signal in the building doesn't stall the form.

2. Their photos go to storage

Up to four. If a photo fails to upload, the submission still goes through without it. A picture problem never costs us the lead.

3. It's saved, and emailed, independently

Deliberately two separate routes. If the database is having a moment, the email still arrives; if email is down, the submission is still saved. Either one alone is enough to not lose the studio.

4. They get a receipt

Only if they left an email address. Worded differently for a new listing than for a change. Sent separately from everything else, so a bounce at their end can never make a good submission look like a failure at ours.

5. It appears in the Inbox

New submissions and change requests in separate sections. The tab shows a count of what's still open.

6. You publish it, or make the change

And the app updates on its own. No deploy, nothing to refresh, nobody to tell.

Two emails go out, not one. One to us with the submission, one to them as a receipt. Both need RESEND_FROM to be set correctly. See when something looks wrong.

3. The admin panel, tab by tab

Five tabs. Here's every one of them and every button on it.

Getting in

Passcode plus your name. The name isn't decoration. It's stamped on every change you make, so the Changes tab can answer "who did that, and when". Your sign-in is remembered for the browser session only, so closing the tab signs you out.

Studios

The main grid. One row per studio, and the cells are directly editable, so click into a name or a service and type. Changed rows turn amber and the bar at the bottom counts them up; nothing is written until you press Save changes, and Discard throws away everything unsaved.

Filter and search
Narrow by shopping center, or type to search names and suites.
Status
live on the app · draft being worked on, not public · hidden off the app but still on file. Hidden rows appear faded with an off the app tag, so they can't be mistaken for live ones.
Tier
What that studio is allowed to show. See tiers.
Details
Opens the full drawer: bio, hours, services, phone, email, booking link, socials, website, photos and their posting code.
Hide / Restore
Hide takes a studio off the app and keeps everything. Restore brings it back as a draft, so you get a look at it before it goes public again.
Remove
Only appears on a row you've just added and haven't saved. It discards the unsaved row. There is no delete for a real studio, on purpose. Hide instead.
Hiding is always the right move over deleting. A studio that leaves the building often comes back, and their photos, hours and code all survive a hide.

Inside the Details drawer

Bio and services
Services are comma separated, and they double as the list a studio can build an offer from so a studio that can't find "balayage" in the offer dropdown is missing it here.
Phone and email
Phone numbers are tidied into (602) 555‑1234 for you. Okay to text and show email control what the public card offers.
Socials and website
Instagram, Facebook, TikTok, website. Handles or full links both work. Each one becomes a round icon button on the card, and only the ones with something in them appear.
Offer code
That studio's posting code, with a button to copy just the code and one to copy the whole ready-to-send message. See offer codes.
Photos
Tap any photo to promote it to the profile shot. Paste an image URL to add one. See photos.

Inbox

Everything that's come in through the forms, in two sections that look deliberately different because they're different jobs.

Change requests

Clay-coloured, with what they want changed in a box at the top rather than buried in the details. Underneath:

  • Open [studio name] jumps straight to that studio's drawer. The panel matches on suite and building, so this button also quietly proves the suite they gave is real.
  • Mark done closes the request once you've made the change. It doesn't make the change itself; it records that somebody dealt with it.
If it says "no live studio in that suite": either they typed the suite wrong, or their listing is currently hidden. Check the grid before assuming they got it wrong. A studio coming back from hidden will always trip this.

New submissions

Pick the shopping center, confirm the suite, then Publish to directory. That creates the studio, links it back to the submission it came from, and, if the tier allows offers, mints their posting code and shows it to you immediately.

Already dealt with

Nothing is deleted. Tick Also show the ones already dealt with to see the history, greyed out. "What did she actually send us in March?" stays answerable.

The count on the tab only ever counts what's still open, so it never nags about finished work.

Offers

Two things live here.

Live offers is what studios have posted. Nothing needs approving and nothing needs taking down; every offer expires on its own. Hide is there for the rare case of something going up that shouldn't have.

Everyone's posting code is a searchable list of every studio's code. Search by suite or name, then copy the code alone or the whole message ready to text. This is the answer to "what's Suite 4417's code again?" without asking anyone.

Settings

What each tier gets
The actual rule, not a description of one. Turn photos off for a tier and those cards lose their photos on the next load, with no deploy. See tiers.
Offer codes
A written explainer of what a code is and when it matters.
Email
Send a test email sends one message to whoever notifications go to and shows exactly what the mail service said back, along with which settings are configured. This is the first thing to press if anyone reports not getting emails.
Shopping centers
A center can hold listings before anyone has traced its floor plan. It just won't have a "show me the way" until the map exists.

Changes

A running log of who changed what and when. Most useful for answering "did I already do that?" and for spotting an accidental edit. It keeps the most recent entries, not the whole history.

4. Tiers: what a studio does and doesn't get

Every studio sits on a tier, and the tier decides what their card is allowed to show. This is enforced on the server, not in the page. Anything a tier doesn't allow is stripped out before it ever reaches a customer's phone. So a tier change takes effect on the next load, everywhere, with nothing to deploy.

SwitchWhat it controls
Photos maxTotal pictures, profile shot included. Set to 4 and they get one profile plus three in the gallery. Set to 0 and their card shows initials.
Can post offersWhether they get a working posting code at all.
ContactWhether the call, text and email buttons appear.
BookingWhether their booking link shows.
SocialsWhether the Instagram, Facebook, TikTok and website buttons show.
BioWhether their written description shows.
HoursWhether opening times show, and therefore whether "Open now" can ever appear.
Changing a tier changes real cards immediately. Turning photos off for a tier doesn't delete anyone's photos. They're still on file and come straight back when you turn it on again, but the public cards do change on the next load.

5. Offer codes

A code is a short password belonging to one studio, like DEUCES-4417. It lets that studio post its own offers at the offer form and does nothing else at all. It can't see other studios, can't edit their listing, and can't reach the admin panel.

Where it comes from
Made automatically the first time a studio is saved on a tier that allows offers. Nobody writes one by hand. It's readable enough to say down a phone, random enough not to be guessed, and deliberately not built from anything printed on their business card.
Where to find it
Two places. On the studio itself, in the Details drawer. Or all of them together under Offers → Everyone's posting code, searchable by suite or name.
When you care
Twice, ever. When you first hand it to a studio, and again if they lose it.
When you can ignore it
Every other time. A studio that never posts an offer simply never uses its code. Codes don't expire and there's nothing to renew.
Dropping a tier
Move a studio to a tier with offers switched off and its code stops working immediately. Move it back and the same code works again.
If one leaks
Worst case is a fake offer on that one studio's card. Hide the offer on the Offers tab, then move the studio to a tier without offers and back again. That mints a fresh code and the old one stops working.
Safe to text. A code only ever lets one studio post its own specials, so there's no need to be precious about how it travels.

6. Photos

Every studio gets one profile shot, the big picture at the top of their card, plus a small gallery underneath. On the top tier that's one profile plus three gallery photos, four in total, which is exactly what the form now accepts.

Which one is the profile
Whichever the studio put first. In the drawer, tap any photo to promote it.
Cropped or whole
Profile shots are cropped to fill by default. Tick show the profile image whole for anything that mustn't be cropped: a business card, a logo, a price list.
Adding one yourself
Paste an image URL into the drawer and press Enter. Photos that arrived with a submission are already hosted, so you can right-click one in the Inbox, copy its address and paste it in.
Why a card shows initials
Either the studio has no photo, or their tier has photos switched off. The drawer says which.
Photos sent with a change request aren't applied automatically. Somebody has to put them on the listing. That's on purpose for now: an automatic swap would let a blurry photo replace a good one with nobody looking.

7. How to do the usual jobs

Put a new studio on the app

  1. Inbox → find them under New submissions.
  2. Check the shopping center and the suite number against the map.
  3. Publish to directory. Their code appears if their tier allows offers, so copy it now.
  4. Studios → Details → tidy the bio, set the profile photo, save.

Handle a change request

  1. Inbox → read the box at the top of the clay card. That's what they actually asked for.
  2. Open [studio] to jump to their listing.
  3. Make the change. If they sent photos, copy the image address from the Inbox card and paste it into the drawer.
  4. Save, then go back and Mark done.

Give a studio their offer code

  1. Offers → Everyone's posting code.
  2. Search their suite or name.
  3. Copy message and paste it into a text. It already explains what to do with it.

A studio has left the building

  1. Studios → Hide.
  2. That's it. They're off the app, everything is kept, and Restore brings them back as a draft if they return.

Switch a studio's "Open now" badge on

  1. Their hours field needs actual opening and closing times, not just days.
  2. Their tier must have hours switched on.
  3. Both true, and the badge appears by itself.

Chase the studios who still need something

  1. Open the building manager page at /laura/.
  2. The who still needs a nudge list names them, grouped by what's missing.
  3. Tap a name to copy a message with their suite already filled in.

8. When something looks wrong

Nobody is getting emails
Settings → Send a test email. It shows which settings are present and exactly what the mail service replied. The usual cause is RESEND_FROM not being set to an address on a verified domain. Without it, mail is refused outright rather than quietly going out from a shared test sender that only ever reaches the account owner.
A studio says the form didn't work
Check the Inbox with show the ones already dealt with ticked, because it may already be published. If it isn't there at all, they most likely didn't press the final button. Their receipt email is the proof it went through, so ask whether they got one.
A change request points at no studio
They typed the wrong suite, or their listing is hidden. Search the grid by name before going back to them.
A card shows initials instead of a photo
No photo on file, or the tier has photos switched off. The Details drawer tells you which.
Socials aren't showing
Nothing in those fields, or the tier has socials switched off.
The app looks out of date
The app paints a built-in copy of the roster first so the building is never blank, then replaces it with live data a moment later. If it stays on the old version, the live call is failing, and the panel loading correctly is the quickest way to tell the difference.
An offer is up that shouldn't be
Offers → Hide. Then see if one leaks.
An edit didn't stick
Grid edits aren't saved until you press Save changes. Amber rows are unsaved.

9. Worth doing now and then

  • Clear the Inbox. The tab count is only honest if things get marked done.
  • Glance at the nudge list on the building manager page. It's the shortest route from "we should chase people" to an actual sent text.
  • Send a test email after any change to mail settings, and once in a while regardless. Mail fails silently by nature.
  • Check the Changes tab if something looks different from how you left it.
  • Look at the app on a phone, not just a laptop. It's the only way anyone actually uses it.
If in doubt, hide rather than delete, and ask rather than guess. Almost nothing in this system is destructive, and the few things that are, are the ones worth a second's pause.
